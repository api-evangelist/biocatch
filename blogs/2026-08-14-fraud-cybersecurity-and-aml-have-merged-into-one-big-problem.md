For the last decade, fraud, cybersecurity, and money laundering were genuinely different problems, confined within their own domains and protected against by unique systems each managed by teams with specific expertise. Fraud teams managed origination and transaction controls, cybersecurity teams protected system integrity and device security, and AML teams reviewed suspicious activity retrospectively, looking for patterns indicative of money laundering.
